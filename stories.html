Stories

Each story follows one character through a day. The learner reads a scene, makes a choice where the story branches, and answers a short quiz. Six stories are listed below, across five languages and three countries.

Wanjiru's Market Day

Kikuyu

Spend a day with Wanjiru: greetings at sunrise, shopping at the market, goodbye at sunset. Kenya.

Mutiso's Harvest Day

Kamba

Help Mutiso bring in the harvest: greetings, counting and sharing. Kenya.

Amina's Mombasa Day

Swahili

A day with Amina: Fort Jesus, coconuts and the ocean. Kenya.

Ochieng's Fishing Morning

Dholuo

Join Ochieng on Lake Victoria: boats, tilapia and blessings. Kenya.

Nakato's Kampala Market

Luganda

Brave Kampala's busy markets with Nakato. Uganda.

Zanzibar Spice Adventure

Swahili

Smell the cloves on Zanzibar's spice farms with Zuhura. Tanzania.
