Learn a language by living a story

MotherTalk is a story-driven web app for learning indigenous African languages. It is built for Gen Z and young urban adults who are at risk of losing those languages. Instead of word lists alone, a learner follows a character through a day, hears the language, and answers short quizzes along the way.

Stories

Six stories across five languages and three countries, from a market day in Kenya to a spice farm in Zanzibar.

Colouring Studio

Colour 30 African-themed pictures, save your work, and earn XP for each saved artwork.

Progress

Earn XP, keep a daily streak, and climb from Mgeni to Mzee. The daily goal is 30 XP.
